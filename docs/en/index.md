!!! note "Made for Project Zomboid"
    Water Pipes is an unofficial **Project Zomboid Build 42.15+ mod**. It expands base building with liquid transport, storage, pressure control, purification, irrigation, and generator refuelling.

## One network, from source to destination

<div class="wp-features" markdown>
<div markdown>
### :material-hammer-wrench: Build

Lay floor, vertical, and concealed pipes, or [plumb a whole building](building-plumbing.md).
</div>
<div markdown>
### :material-water: Supply and store

Connect containers, rain collectors, wells, rivers, municipal water, and hydrants. See [sources and storage](sources-and-storage.md).
</div>
<div markdown>
### :material-valve: Control

Manage direction, isolation, and pressure with pumps, routers, valves, and gauges.
</div>
<div markdown>
### :material-faucet: Use

Supply fixtures, purify water, irrigate crops, or deliver petrol to generators.
</div>
</div>
